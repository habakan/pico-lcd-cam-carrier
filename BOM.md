## Where the money goes

| | Share of about $30 |
|---|---:|
| LCD HAT | 47% |
| Pico 2 H | 22% |
| Camera | 20% |
| Board, connectors, screws, case | 11% |

The screen and the camera are two thirds of the cost. A build that reads PSBTs from a microSD card instead
of the camera, with a smaller screen, is where the cost comes down.

## Not in the table

- Shipping and tax, which are often more than the board itself for a single order.
- Fitting the connectors at the factory. Rev E was ordered with J1-J4 fitted, so builders need no soldering;
  that assembly fee will be added from the order.
- A USB cable to flash the firmware and power the device.

Prices vary by region and store, and some of these parts are hard to find in some countries.
